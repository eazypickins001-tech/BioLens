# 04 — CAPTIONS, HASHTAGS & PINNED COMMENTS

> Filled once for BioLens. Every post reuses the structure; only the hook line
> and body change.

---

## 1. Caption rules

1. **The first line is the hook.** Same punch as the on-screen text, rewritten
   for the platform. Never start with context.
2. **Short lines.** One idea per line. Line breaks are pacing.
3. **No hashtags in the middle of a sentence.** Hashtags go at the end of the
   caption or in the first comment — pick one and stay consistent.
4. **The link goes in the bio on Instagram/TikTok**, and in the caption on
   YouTube/Facebook.
5. **One CTA.** Never "follow, like, share and comment".
6. **The caption must still work if the video never plays.**
7. **No claim without a source** in the description when the number or mechanism
   carries the hook.

## 3. Hashtag strategy

Use **8–12** per post. Three tiers, every time.

| Tier | Purpose | BioLens tags |
|---|---|---|
| **Reach** (high volume) | Discovery | `#biology` `#nature` `#science` `#shorts` `#fyp` |
| **Creator / niche audience** | The people who stay | `#microbiology` `#zoology` `#anatomy` `#wildlife` `#entomology` |
| **Niche / on-brand** | Identity and topic signal | `#biolens` `#seelifedifferently` `#macrophotography` `#hiddenbiology` `#natureisalien` |
| **Brand** (always) | Channel memory | `#BioLens` |

Rules:

- Keep the same core set so the algorithm learns the topic.
- Rotate 2–3 reach tags per post to test.
- Never use a hashtag the content does not earn. A plant post does not get
  `#anatomy`.
- Maximum 12. Minimum 8.

**Project hashtag set:**

```
REACH:   #biology #nature #science #shorts #fyp
CREATOR: #microbiology #zoology #anatomy #wildlife #entomology
NICHE:   #biolens #seelifedifferently #macrophotography #hiddenbiology #natureisalien
BRAND:   #BioLens
```

## 4. Pinned comments

Every post gets one pinned comment, written before publishing. Its job is to
start the thread, not to summarise the post.

Patterns that work for BioLens:

- **The source:** "The paper is linked in the description — read the abstract
  before you decide I'm wrong."
- **The near-miss:** "The first render had the scale wrong. The ruler in the
  corner is the correction."
- **The invitation:** "Tell me the biology fact you refused to believe at first."
- **The sequel:** "There is a second mechanism in this animal that didn't fit in
  32 seconds. Want it?"

**Do not** pin "Thanks for watching" or anything that ends the conversation.

---

## 5. Comment seeding (three per post)

Each post ships with three comments in `03_POST_BANK.md`. They must be:

1. **Specific** — about this post, not any post.
2. **Answerable** — a viewer can reply in five words.
3. **Non-promotional** — they invite, they do not sell.

Weak → strong:

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| "Great video!" | "Did you catch the moment the genome reassembles?" |
| "Thoughts?" | "Which part sounds more impossible: the tapping or the listening?" |
| "Follow for more" | "What would you test first if you could put this under a lens?" |

---

## 6. Publishing rhythm

- Match the cadence in `00_DNA.md`: 5 posts per week, Monday to Friday.
- Strongest post of the week goes out first.
- Stagger shorts from the same research across the week.
- Never two salesy posts back to back.
- Post, then pin the comment immediately.
- Reply to every comment for the first 90 days.
